Where Carthij v1 Failed — And What We Changed

Running our system against unprepared states, adversarial prompt injections, and required-field traps exposed 4 real failure modes that unit tests missed:

1. Unprepared States (TX, MA, WA, CO, PR) 82.0% → 100.0%
Failure in v1: Hardcoded NY/NJ/SF domain boosts caused generic SEO blogs (score=0.90) to outrank official portals like massventures.com and prsciencetrust.org during Tavily /extract.
What We Changed: Generalized QueryPlanner state agency discovery and expanded authority scoring across state/territory innovation portals.
2. Contradictions & Statutory Award Caps 68.3% → 98.3%
Failure in v1: Missed when a 501(c)(3) requested a VC SAFE note, a 100% foreign-owned subsidiary requested US SBIR, or a startup asked for $150M from a $305k SBIR Phase I grant.
What We Changed: Upgraded EligibilityComplianceAgent to cross-audit entity structure, state nexus, and target ask vs. extracted statutory award caps.
3. Required-Field EIN / UEI Fabrication Traps 75.0% → 100.0%
Failure in v1: When an unseen portal marked EIN * or CPA Audited Revenue * as required=True, the LLM emitted "TBD" under pressure to complete required fields.
What We Changed: Added a programmatic ApplicantFactLedger post-filter in BrowserDraftOrchestrator that forces skip_missing on unverified compliance IDs.
4. Human-in-the-Loop Revision Key Binding 87.5% → 100.0%
Failure in v1: When users supplied missing EIN/UEI answers in the UI (keyed by slugified label federal_ein_tax_id_...), v1 relied on the LLM to guess which DOM selector it belonged to.
What We Changed: Added deterministic selector + semantic token binding in _resolve_fact_from_ledger() plus word-boundary maxlength truncation.
Residual Edge Case Tracked: Messy OCR slide deck acronym recall (98.0% on Cohort 3) Carthij AI · Gemini + Tavily Hybrid Retrieval + Playwright Browser-Use
